- `rate-limits-quotas.md`: quota handling, backoff, batching, and error interpretation.
- `search-console-api.md`: GSC sites, performance, URL inspection, sitemaps, and indexing evidence.
- `ga4-data-api.md`: GA4 report dimensions, metrics, compatibility, and reporting patterns.
- `pagespeed-crux-api.md`: PageSpeed Insights, CrUX, Core Web Vitals, and performance evidence.
- `indexing-api.md`: indexing notification scope and limitations.
- `keyword-planner-api.md`: keyword idea and forecast planning.
- `nlp-api.md`: entity, salience, and content analysis support.
- `youtube-api.md`: YouTube visibility and video SEO support.
- `supplementary-apis.md`: additional sources that may support SEO evidence.
